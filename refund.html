Refund policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

14-day refund

If your full report isn't useful to you, ask for a refund within 14 days of purchase and we will refund the full $19.95 CAD to your original payment method. No questions asked, one refund per person.

How to ask

Reply to your Stripe receipt, or use the support email shown on it, with the date of purchase. Refunds are usually processed within 5 business days; your bank may take a few more days to show it.

After a refund

Your report link stops working and your report is deleted.

Free updates

Every purchase includes free updates to your answers for 90 days. Updates are part of the original purchase and are not charged separately.

Prices

Prices are in Canadian dollars. Applicable sales taxes may be added at checkout.
