Privacy policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

The short version

We collect only your questionnaire answers, to build your report. We never ask for your name. Payment is handled by Stripe; we never see your card. You can delete your report at any time from inside it.

What we collect

  • Questionnaire answers. Your goals, activity, age range, optional body weight, diet, sleep, stress and mood answers, health conditions, medicine groups, pregnancy status and whether you compete in a drug-tested sport. Some of this is health information, which we treat as sensitive.
  • Payment details. If you buy a report, Stripe collects your email and payment details to process the payment and send a receipt. We receive confirmation that you paid and a payment reference, not your card number.
  • Optional research answers. If you choose “Send anonymously” in your report, a copy of your answers without weight, name or contact details is stored separately for research. It cannot be linked back to your report.
  • On your device. Your browser stores your private report link so you can return to it. We use no advertising or tracking cookies and no analytics.

How we use it

Only to generate, show and update your report, to confirm payment, and, for anonymous research answers, to improve the questions and content. We do not sell or rent your information and do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Reports are stored in a Supabase database hosted in Canada (Montréal region). Payments are processed by Stripe, which may process data outside Canada under its own privacy policy. The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages.

How long we keep it

  • Unpaid previews are deleted automatically after 7 days.
  • Paid reports are kept so your private link keeps working, until you delete them.
  • Anonymous research answers are kept until no longer needed.
  • Stripe keeps payment records as required by law.

Your private link

Your report is protected by a long random key in your private link. Anyone who has the link can open the report, so keep it to yourself. We store only a scrambled (hashed) version of the key.

Your choices and rights

You can delete your report at any time with the “Delete my report” button inside it. Under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA) you can ask to access or correct your information, withdraw consent, or complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Not medical advice

Supplement Check is an educational tool. It does not diagnose or treat any condition and is not a substitute for advice from your doctor, pharmacist or dietitian.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, reply to your Stripe receipt or use the support email shown on it.
